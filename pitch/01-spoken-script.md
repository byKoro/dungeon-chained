# Dungeon Chained — Spoken Script (Pitch Narration)

> **Idioma da narração:** inglês (decisão de produção). Este documento é o texto de locução.
> **Alvo de duração:** 2min30 a 3min (limite da Game Jam Plus: 5min).
> **Como ler:** texto corrido natural para gravar. `[PAUSE]` = respiro. **negrito** = ênfase vocal.
> **Arma:** apenas a serra (saw). Nunca mencionar laser.

---

## BLOCK 1 — Cold Open / Hook (Lore) — ~22s · acc 0:22

> Tom: sombrio, mítico, baixo e grave. Começa quase sussurrado, cresce na última linha.

Two brothers. The greatest smiths the gods ever knew.
**Brokkr and Eitri** forged Thor's hammer — and they **died** trying to defend him. [PAUSE]
Now they wake up somewhere worse than death: a **Norse hell**.
And the goddess **Hela** has stolen Mjölnir.
So the brothers do the only thing smiths can do. They **forge**.
One last weapon — a **saw** — and it **chains them together**. [PAUSE]
To get the hammer back, they'll have to cut their way out. **Together. Whether they like it or not.**

---

## BLOCK 2 — Problem + Audience — ~20s · acc 0:42

> Tom: direto, confiante, muda de "mítico" para "pitch". Energia de produto.

This is **Dungeon Chained** — a **two-player couch co-op roguelike**.
One keyboard. Two friends. One chain they **cannot** escape.
It's built for the people who love playing **shoulder to shoulder** — couples, roommates, best friends.
And it's built for **streamers**, because nothing goes viral faster than two friends **screaming at each other** over a shared chain. [PAUSE]

---

## BLOCK 3 — The Game & The Saw (Core Mechanic) — ~32s · acc 1:14

> Tom: empolgado, explicando o truque. Pausa antes da frase-gancho.

Here's the hook. The saw the brothers forged **isn't just a weapon**.
It **is** the chain. [PAUSE]
A line of **spinning blades** runs between the two players — always between them.
Walk apart, and the chain pulls **taut**. Line up the enemies, and the blades **shred** everything in the middle.
So your weapon's reach, your movement, your safety — all of it depends on **where your partner stands**.
You don't aim the saw. You **position each other**. The chain is your sword, your rope, and your leash — **all at once**.

---

## BLOCK 4 — Conflict Co-op (The Friction) — ~24s · acc 1:38

> Tom: ritmo mais rápido, divertido, caótico. Cortes rápidos na imagem.

And that's where the **chaos** begins.
You want to go left. Your partner **bolts** right — and drags you straight into the **spikes**.
There's a pit between you and the exit, and the chain is pulling your friend **over the edge**. [PAUSE]
Doors only open when **both** of you step through. Pressure plates only trigger when you **both** commit.
Dungeon Chained isn't cooperative because you're nice. It's cooperative because the **chain gives you no choice**.

---

## BLOCK 5 — Inspirations & Value — ~30s · acc 2:08

> Tom: credível, "nós sabemos o mercado". Nomear os jogos com respeito.

We stand on the shoulders of games that **proved this works**.
The furious, friendship-testing chaos of **Overcooked**.
The endless, never-the-same runs of **The Binding of Isaac** — our dungeon is **procedural**, so every escape is different.
The emotional, built-for-two co-op that made **It Takes Two** a Game of the Year.
And the single, physical bond that drove **Chained Together**.
Dungeon Chained takes the **best of all four** — the fight, the replayability, the togetherness, the leash — and forges them into **one blade**.

---

## BLOCK 6 — Market / Business — ~20s · acc 2:28

> Tom: executivo, mas vivo. Isto é o bloco "para o investidor".

Co-op is one of the **stickiest** genres in gaming — friends buy **two copies**, play for hours, and bring each other in.
Our model is simple: an **accessible premium** price for a pair, and growth driven by **streamers and content** — because the chain **manufactures clips**.
Low cost to reach. High reason to share.

---

## BLOCK 7 — Roadmap / Vision — ~20s · acc 2:48

> Tom: visionário, crescente.

What you're seeing is a **playable vertical slice**. Here's where it's going.
Real **boss fights** at the end of each descent. **Roguelike meta-progression** between runs. **More saws, more rooms, more enemies.**
Ongoing **content expansions** and **new maps** to keep the dungeon alive.
And one day — **online co-op**, so the chain can stretch across the world.

---

## BLOCK 8 — Call to Action — ~12s · acc 3:00

> Tom: fecha forte, volta ao mítico por um segundo, depois o CTA limpo.

Two brothers. One saw. No way out but **together**. [PAUSE]
**Dungeon Chained.**
Wishlist it. Follow the forge. And grab a friend — you're going to need one.
